The deal

Fair play

One puzzle a day, the same one for everybody. Don't try to pull the answer out of the server, automate guesses, or spoil the day's parse for people who haven't played yet. Accounts that do can be removed from the game.

The game is provided as is, with no guarantee that it will be available, correct, or free of bugs. Parse data comes from Warcraft Logs and is only as accurate as what was uploaded. Scores, streaks and stored results may be lost or reset.

Parsedle is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment, Warcraft Logs, or Discord.

Ending it

Stop playing whenever you like; ask an admin to delete your account and results and they will. The admins may also shut the game down or remove access at any time.
